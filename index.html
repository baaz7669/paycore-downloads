About PayCore

Most payroll software is sold as a subscription priced per employee. As your team grows, so does the bill, every month, for as long as you use it.

PayCore was built to change that. One license covers unlimited employees, the software runs on your own computer, and your payroll data never leaves your control.

Development and support are handled directly by the PayCore team, so every question is answered by the people who build the software.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a monthly fee?

No. PayCore is a one-time license, with no subscription and no per-employee charges.

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. We offer a free live demo by video call. Contact us on WhatsApp or by email to arrange a time.

Does it need the internet?

No. Payroll, reports, backups and activation all work offline. Only the optional AI assistant uses the internet, with your own Google Gemini API key.

Can I move it to a new computer?

Yes. Install PayCore on the new computer, activate it with the same email and license key, and restore your data from a backup.

Does it handle payroll in Oman and the Gulf?

Yes. Omani Rial, Kuwaiti Dinar and Bahraini Dinar use 3 decimals, and AED, SAR and QAR are built in. PayCore also has a full Arabic interface, end-of-service gratuity with your own rate, and WPS bank-transfer rules for each employee.

What if I lose my license key?

Contact us with the email address the license was registered to, and we'll send it again.

Is my data safe?

Your payroll data stays on your computer and is never sent to us or any cloud service. Access is protected by a PIN. Backup files are not encrypted, so store them securely.
